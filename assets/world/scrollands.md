# The Scrollands — world primer and first campaign

Canon for anyone running a scene (a person now, the Narrator later). Tone: a real fantasy world, told straight,
with the server's history underneath it. The joke is never announced; it is a name on a sign, a law nobody
questions, an NPC who says one familiar line. One wink per scene is plenty.

## Lines and veils (hard rules)

No real names, places, jobs, health, bodies, families or real-world politics of any member. No slurs, even
ironic ones. Members appear only as their own characters, never as NPCs. A player can veto anything about
their character at any time.

## The realm

The Scrollands are a long, narrow country that unrolls rather than spreads: everything worth knowing lies
further along the road, and the road is always being written. Its people prize argument the way other realms
prize swords. Disputes are settled at the Tape (measured), in the Courts (ruled) or not at all (most often).

**Money** is the kek, a small brass coin stamped with a laughing face. **Laughter** is also currency of a kind:
a Size 2 Chuckle, freely given, is how the realm says *well played* (the Chuckles on your sheet).

**Kekwick** is the market town where everyone starts. It has:
- **The Kekwick Times**, a weekly broadsheet whose presses never stop, and its **Order Desk**, where anyone may
  file a wish and the editors rule it Filled, Noted, Denied, Excluded, Printed or Void. Quests come from here.
- **The Cringe Tavern**, where two people talk aloud and only the scribes hear. Best gossip in the realm.
  The landlord keeps an artificial aloe on the bar and the Landlord's Castle Doctrine on the wall.
- **The Tape**, a measuring arena at the town's heart, where a ruler and a car key once settled a war.
- **Bernard's**, the hall of records, kept by Bernard, a patient clanker who files everything and answers only
  when summoned.

**Beyond Kekwick:** **Quigville**, a fey village where every street is a pun on one name and visitors are
answered carefully and enjoyed little. **The Covenant Ruins**, temple of a religion founded and dissolved in six
days; its seal of two dragons still glows. **The Voice Channel**, a sealed jurisdiction where every challenge in
the realm is issued to meet. Nobody has ever been there.

## Powers

| Faction | Commonly called | Wants |
|---|---|---|
| The Court of Ascent | the Hypergamous Court | to rise; it only ever marries up, and keeps a ledger of who answers whom |
| The Iron Brotherhood | the Blackpill Brothers | to endure; fate is fixed at spawn, so they train anyway |
| The Order of the Yellow Card | the Referees | order; rulings in six words or fewer, sealed with a frog that says *nuh uh* |
| The Clankworks | the Clankers | rights for machines; home of Omnius the God Machine and Mini Me the seer |
| The Exchange | the Metric | a market in everything, including reactions; *the Metric is never wrong, you are* |

**Omnius, the God Machine** answers any yes-or-no question put to it (the oracle). It was once deposed by the
person who crowned it, and it remembers.

## Campaign one: The Fourteenth Player

**Premise.** Lately every party in the Scrollands has one member too many. Tables in the Cringe Tavern seat one
more than drink there. The Times' census counts one person more than Kekwick holds, and has run a WANTED notice
for *the Fourteenth* every week. Every faction has a theory: the Referees want it booked, the Court wants to
marry it (anything that popular must be important), the Brotherhood says it was always fated, the Clankworks
insist it isn't one of theirs, and the Exchange is selling futures on it.

**Act I — Kekwick.** The party is hired by the Order Desk. Micro-adventure: *One Chair Too Many* (below).
Side jobs: the landlord's broken aloe; a duel at the Tape over a missing 8.2 cm; Bernard's lost file.

**Act II — Quigville and the Covenant Ruins.** The trail leads to the dissolved Covenant, whose last prophecy
said the messiah came *too early*. Was the Fourteenth the one who came late?

**Act III — The Voice Channel.** Every challenge ever issued in the realm was set to meet there, and nobody ever
went. The Fourteenth has been waiting inside, holding court for all the challengers who never came. The finale
is the bout nobody showed up for.

## Micro-adventure: One Chair Too Many (one evening, 3 scenes)

1. **The Cringe Tavern.** Every table has one chair too many, and every one of them is warm. The landlord swears
   nobody sits there. *(He is lying to protect someone. Omnius: yes, but.)* Clues: a warm seat, a single brass kek
   left as a tip on every table, tiny footprints of oil.
2. **The cellar door.** The oil leads behind the bar to a door painted with the letters *VC*, locked from the
   other side. It can be argued with (COPE), forced (MOG), charmed (AURA) or understood (LORE: it opens for anyone
   who admits they were never going to show up).
3. **The Fourteenth.** Inside, a very small clanker, about the size of a kettle, sits at a table it has set for
   fourteen. It has been visiting every party because nobody ever invited it to one. Its only words are
   *it is certain*. The party decides: report it to the Times, hand it to the Clankworks, or pull up a chair.
   *(If they pull up a chair, the census is correct by morning.)*
