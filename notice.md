**Personas**
- New: jere, aura, dot and ape. `/persona` opens grouped (chatters, characters, tools); `/persona-manage` does create/edit/delete/pictures
- Personas now see the parts of the channel that matter to your message, and can tell members apart from the bot

**Tools:** start a mention with the name, or just ask
- **debate review**: `@Mechirk-Fucking-Epstein who won that?`

- **steelman**: `@Mechirk-Fucking-Epstein steelman both sides`

**Quick answers** (instant, no GPU)
- `/quick decide`, `odds`, `tier`, `rate`, `tone`, `vibe`, `chatter`, or just ask: `@Mechirk-Fucking-Epstein vibe check`

**Duels**
- `/duel aura dot is a hot dog a sandwich`: two personas argue, Jev judges

**Scores**
- `/scores debates`, `/scores duels`; react 🔮 on a message to log a prediction, settle it with `/scores predictions`. Receipts are forever.

**Also**
- Shorter replies; the GPU stays awake five minutes after a reply

- Asking the same thing twice gets a link to the first answer
